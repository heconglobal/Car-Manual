# Body R8 exterior review

Source: `0c1e23dd80f0886b27d6e772e2800d88db8c74418c309c5d59a62a201255d310`.

Review scope: the complete exterior of the 1985 non-aero SE notchback, using the Pontiac MVMA dimensions, original brochure/parts illustrations and owner photographs. [Specification and region-by-region record](../references/body-r8-specification.md). Every capture counted in the [visual manifest](visual-inspection-manifest.json) has been opened; development captures from earlier sources are archived separately.

## Observed corrections

- The nose now has larger recessed black lamp wells, correctly centered amber lenses within the pads, an exposed lower deflector, fitted wraparound molding and a crest seated on the sloped skin. Closed/raised headlamp openings remain distinct.
- Both side elevations retain the factory-length and axle envelope. The molding line is continuous across the door/quarter transition. Fuel and intake openings have defined cut boundaries; the intake is below the molding. Door-gap backing prevents background sightlines through the seams.
- Roof, windshield, side glass, rear header and notchback pillars were inspected in full and close views. The roof return and pillar crown now share an edge. The sail applique retains its separate opaque frame; its finish is less metallic.
- The deck retains its raised centre and separate open ventilation grilles. Carrier supports and wing pedestals meet their respective foil undersides instead of protruding through the upper surfaces.
- The deeper rear apron, quarter-end fit, exhaust scallops, separate bumper pads, plate recess and softer lamp corners remain. The clear lamp cover permits the red/reverse inserts to read under both review lighting rigs.
- Wheels retain the five-spoke/parallel-fin design with a less reflective face. Sidewall rings follow the tire profile; a continuous grooved tread replaces the toothed outline. The tire lettering reads in the correct order on the inspected passenger side.
- Mirror shells/glass, handles, key cylinders, wipers, antenna, cowl grille, emblems, window seals and rocker returns retain their independent geometry and source references.

## Limits of the review

The 16 nominal dimensional comparisons pass, as do the five focused body audits. Their numerical tolerances are mesh-check tolerances, not production tolerances. Two published bumper ground-reference interpretations remain unresolved. The 78 trim sightlines, nine intake sightlines, 234 carrier contacts and 130 pedestal contacts establish those reconstructed interfaces, not factory tooling.

The exterior is ready for another owner review, not certified as an exact measured replica. Panel lofts, local radii/gaps, optical tooling, weatherstrip sections and fine badge artwork remain reconstructed. Glass and reflections differ between software alpha rendering, hardware transmission and outdoor photography. Thin details remain limited by the whole-car viewport's pixel resolution. Comparison cameras are not calibrated overlays, and the ground/grass in the photographs must not be treated as a measured ride-height plane.

No owner sign-off or full-vehicle acceptance is inferred from this development pass.
