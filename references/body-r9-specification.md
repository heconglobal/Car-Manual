# Body R9 — exterior audit and correction

Scope: 1985 Pontiac Fiero SE 2M6, non-aero notchback, US left-hand drive. Work resumed at the owner's request on 26 September 2026. The R8 pause record remains historical evidence.

## What establishes the specification

- Pontiac 1985–86 MVMA specification, issued 1 September 1984, [PDF22–23](1985-86-specifications.pdf#page=22): selected nominal body dimensions. [PDF26](1985-86-specifications.pdf#page=26) gives lamp locations; load conditions differ from the design-load body dimensions.
- Pontiac [1985 brochure, PDF3](1985-fiero-brochure.pdf#page=3): the original SE non-aero shape and 14-inch Hi-Tech wheels. GT/aero and later fastback illustrations are not substituted for the SE.
- GM 22P [PDF237](fiero-parts-cd.pdf#page=237), front assembly, and [PDF336–338](fiero-parts-cd.pdf#page=336), figure 2P12-012 plus application list: panels, weatherstrip retainers, fillers and mounting relationships. The 1985 rows, rather than every item illustrated in this multi-year plate, determine applicability.
- Pontiac production packaging profile, [PDF37 / printed46](pontiac-super-duty-guide-r7.pdf#page=37): a second silhouette reference. It is not a dimensioned body surface loft.
- Owner photographs IMG_5459/5461/5462 provide secondary appearance evidence. Camera perspective, glass reflections and the grass ground plane are not measurement datums.

The 16 selected nominal comparisons cover 4082 mm length, 1752 mm width, 1192 mm solid-roof height, 924/785 mm overhangs, cowl/deck references, upper-structure length, seat-section width, rocker bottoms and closed door bottoms. Wheelbase remains 2373 mm. Numerical mesh tolerances are not production tolerances. H102/H104 bumper ground-reference interpretations remain open; reconstructed apron minima are not relabeled as those factory datums.

## Parts-accounting corrections

Ten previously grouped or missing exterior opening parts are now independent selections: paired A-pillar seals/retainers, upper-window seals/retainers/stops, lock-pillar seals/retainers, windshield belt fillers and rear-window side fillers. They use the same geometry in the car and Body explorer.

The actual GM 2P12-012 list identifies item **36 as the roof panel** and **38 as the rear fascia**; previous catalog references were reversed. Item **39 is the rear-window side filler**, not the backlight glass. Those source-callout errors are corrected. Items 26–29 establish the additional glazing retainers/fillers; their authored cross sections remain reconstructed.

[Exterior inventory and interface audit](../artifacts/exterior-completeness-audit.json) records explicit expected exterior pieces against real mesh presence. It deliberately records remaining concealed mechanisms and factory tooling gaps. Catalog counts alone do not establish that every physical fastener, seal section or original option is complete.

## R10 follow-up

The owner identified incorrect window seals after R9. [R10 correction](body-r10-window-seals.md) supersedes R9’s seal-fit assessment with formed profiles, shared glass boundaries and independent contact/clear-pane checks. R9’s proximity-only checks did not establish correct seal fit.

## Review and limits

[Body review](http://localhost:5185/body-review.html) now includes orthographic driver/passenger/front/rear elevations and plan view, alongside reference-photo perspectives and wheel/roof/nose close views. Installed body hardware is included in this workspace so it cannot be silently omitted from exterior review. The rear-lamp workspace also includes the separately owned backlight fillers, fuel door and intake grille; its context no longer leaves empty holes where those pieces belong.

Final current-source results are recorded in the focused body audits, browser execution notes and visual-inspection manifest. Intermediate renders are preserved but are not final evidence.

Exact production panel lofts, local radii, weatherstrip profiles, full hidden mechanisms/fastener inventory and original option application remain open. This pass is a correction and review of the exterior reconstruction, not certification of factory tooling accuracy or owner acceptance.
