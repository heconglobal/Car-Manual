# Factory source and component reconciliation, 2026-10-01

The project now has an auditable link between all detailed selections, their current source records, grouped items and unresolved evidence. Run `node scripts/audit-completion-sources.mjs` after source changes to regenerate the [family audit](completion-source-audit.json), [selection ledger](completion-selection-evidence.csv) and [service-page manifest](completion-service-evidence.json). Previous outputs are preserved before each run. A passing audit establishes valid local IDs and PDF page ranges, not a complete or measured vehicle.

The independently inspected [drawing register](completion-callout-register.json) accounts for the ten battery mounting callouts in GM figure 2P02-005, thirteen master-cylinder callouts in GM04-203, and all 97 numbered four-speed table entries in GM04-238. Each entry names the corresponding model IDs or the unresolved gap. The gearbox register deliberately does not reuse the differently numbered 1986 service callouts. Aggregate service assemblies, alternative applications and grouped hardware are identified rather than summed into a misleading parts total. Many other catalog figures still need the same independent drawing-by-drawing treatment.

The original 1985 DIY publication supports thirteen interactive guides, including eleven additions covering oil, filters, transaxle and hydraulic fluid checks, spark plugs, battery and wiper blades/refills. The [source manifest](completion-service-evidence.json) records each step's exact PDF page and selected part. V6-specific plug gap and oil capacity, four-speed capacity, battery cable order and original torque references were read from the original page scans. Four-cylinder-only steps are excluded. The instructions identify unmodeled service access, reconstructed geometry and historical lubricant/product specifications. Physical workshop testing remains pending; the owner has said it will happen later.

The [licensed publisher's S-8510P listing](https://www.detroitironis.com/1985-pontiac-fiero-service-manual-print.html) identifies a 1,146-page chassis/body factory reprint. The [Fieroinfo archive](https://fieroinfo.com/manuals/) provides 1985 DIY, owner and 6E3 engine-control documents but currently lists full service manuals for other model years. This research pass did not find a validated complete free primary 1985 service PDF. The listing and a broad search result are not evidence that the missing procedures have been checked.

The [1985 V6 Operation CHARM tree](https://charm.li/Pontiac/1985/Fiero%20V6-173%202.8L/) is a useful further research lead, with diagrams and bulletins. It also includes generic entries such as tire-pressure monitoring and other-engine systems. Each claimed primary GM page and its application must therefore be checked independently. No blanket original-year verification is assigned to this compiled tree.

`references/1985-owners.pdf` is a zero-page download and remains preserved. The usable scan is `references/1985-owners-boomtastic.pdf`, with 107 pages. The existing `ownersSource` and page-audit map already point to that edition. Its printed 6-2 and 6-4 correspond to PDF pages 90 and 92. Page references must not be transferred between editions without checking them.

Actual paint/trim/RPO labels, spring tags, gearbox identification, accessory stamps and electrical calibration labels remain vehicle-specific evidence to collect later. The owner-reported four-speed and WS6 configuration can guide the software model; it cannot supply missing original part numbers, measured profiles or physical validation.

## Additional wiper callout reconciliation

The independent register now contains 210 callout rows across eight drawings, including GM2P10-008, GM10-066 and GM10-068 (PDF284/286/287). Original page images are preserved in `completion-wiper-callouts/`. Standard1984–85 and CD4 1985–87 applications remain distinct; the broad1984–88 motor-family captions do not establish interchangeability. High-resolution inspection confirms brush-holder22029825 and identifies22062915 as the complete standard motor service number. The front-compartment release handle and original screw-package quantities remain unresolved. These are identity crosswalks, not measured geometry or complete electrical validation.

The spare/jack figure 2P08-009 (PDF 251) adds ten rows, including the 15×4 wheel, separately inspectable jack construction, wrench and stowage hardware. The door figure 2P10-004 (PDF 300–302) adds 54 rows with original year/type splits retained. Door rows 11, 13, 19, 23 and 40 have no independently attributed model selection; striker and washer rows are grouped, and item 34’s preview-option binding remains unresolved. Original application, physical quantities and measured fit are not inferred from selectable inventory.
