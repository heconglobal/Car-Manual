It checks every selection's geometry and picking identity, exclusive standard/pulse alternatives, the two original catalog applications, left/right orientation, bottom-mounted pump and an open bottle filler neck. The browser scenario exercises independent explosion, selection, isolation, option changes, mobile access and return navigation. Its results and screenshots must be assessed after the final source is frozen.

All installation coordinates are reconstructed. Gear tooth counts, reduction ratio, worm lead, pivot coordinates, actual sweep, glass/hood clearance through travel, circuit pinouts, delay/park calibration, pump internals, reservoir capacity and molded markings remain unverified. Grouped service assemblies and repeated contexts are not unique physical part counts. The defroster's complete circuit remains open. These views are not removal procedures or proof of a complete factory-spec assembly.
