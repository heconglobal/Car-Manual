# Engine cooling and lubrication service detail

Research and reconstruction: 2026-09-21. This increment adds 27 selectable engine service parts or explicitly grouped sets. It also replaces the water-pump/pulley envelopes and shares the thermostat geometry with the vehicle view. No reference photographs are loaded by the application.

## References inspected

- GM Pontiac 22P, November 1990, [H-19 drawing / H-20–21 tables](https://fieroinfo.com/manuals/84-88_Fiero_Parts_%26_Illustrations_P22.pdf#page=14): dipstick, tube, mounting screw, seal, filter connector/bypass, intermediate shaft, early pan seal, front seal, timing guide, water pump and early heater fitting. This catalog covers multiple years; its later pan reinforcement and one-piece gasket were excluded.
- GM 22P [H-22 drawing / H-23–24 tables](https://fieroinfo.com/manuals/84-88_Fiero_Parts_%26_Illustrations_P22.pdf#page=17): thermostat housing, mounting gasket/bolts, thermostat/cap, and the A/C oil-pressure pipe arrangement. The 1985–87 sender identity was cross-checked in H-20; the later 1988 sender was excluded.
- Original [1985 Pontiac DIY, printed 2-39, PDF 48](https://fieroinfo.com/manuals/1985_Fiero_Do_It_Yourself.pdf#page=48): thermostat removal illustration establishes the tall open pull handle. Printed 2-41 / PDF 50 identifies the six-cylinder housing at the passenger end and distinguishes the housing cap from the radiator cap.
- [MotoRad 211-195](https://motorad.com/part/211-195/): manufacturer lists 93.22 mm height, 43.69 mm seal outside diameter, 65.28 mm height between flanges, and 195 °F opening temperature. These are **replacement reference dimensions**, not measurements of this car's factory thermostat. The GM H-24 rating is independently 195 °F. The manufacturer HTML was readable through web retrieval; direct image retrieval failed with HTTP 403, so no product photograph was inspected or imported. The unrelated MotoRad 200-195 and four-cylinder housing 10038440 were excluded.

## Geometry and interaction

- The thermostat neck has a hollow bore, side opening, hose bead, rolled filler lip, flange opening and bolt holes. Cap, thermostat, housing, gasket and bolts separate independently. The tall handle is a thin stamped arch; the spring and copper capsule are modeled geometry. Its seal diameter and overall envelope use the expressly labeled replacement reference. The original stamped profiles, cap details and housing dimensions remain reconstructed.
- The pump has a contoured cast volute, bearing nose, hub, irregular mounting flange and hollow rear cavity. Pulley, mounting gasket, early heater fitting, mounting bolts and pulley bolts are separate selections. The sealed pump is not falsely exploded into unverified bearing/impeller internals.
- The dipstick is a swept flat blade, with a formed loop and stopper. Its separate guide is a hollow sweep, with mouth flare, bracket, fastener and seal. Length, route and level markings are illustrative, not an oil-level calibration.
- The sender scope explicitly previews the **A/C** pipe arrangement, independently of the vehicle options. It does not claim that VIN identifies A/C. The early sender is a large molded body over a metal base, with separate pipe/adapter/support fastener. The non-A/C arrangement is still missing.
- Timing-cover gasket, front crank seal, timing pointer and chain guide are independently selectable. Exact timing marks, wear profile and gasket outlines are not specifications.
- The pan's drain plug and flange bolts are no longer fused to the pan selection. Side-rail gaskets replace the former closed rectangular gasket loop; the early rear end seal is separate. Exact early-pan flange/end-seal fit and front sealing still need further reconstruction.

## Remaining accuracy work

The new geometry improves component identity and surface detail. It is not measured factory CAD or a complete photorealistic engine. Engine casting dimensions, mechanical alignments, actual fastener lengths, pump internals, precise hose routes, the complete early-pan joint, and the non-A/C sender installation remain incomplete. Catalog counts include service sets and overlap the vehicle view; they are not a whole-car part count.
