# 1985 SE fascia and spoiler refinement

September 17, 2026. This pass addresses the owner's feedback that the front and rear looked too square and the body lines too rigid. It refines visible geometry; it does not establish aerodynamic performance or measured surface accuracy.

## Reference evidence

- [1985 Pontiac Fiero Canadian brochure](https://xr793.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/1985-Pontiac-Fiero-Cdn.pdf), PDF page 3: SE front and side proportions, sloping nose, rounded corners and luggage carrier. Page 2 shows the separate GT pedestal-wing configuration.
- [GM Pontiac 22P parts catalog](https://fieroinfo.com/manuals/84-88_Fiero_Parts_%26_Illustrations_P22.pdf), November 1990 edition:
  - G-13, PDF page 223: non-WU2 1985 front fascia, wraparound upper molding, paired pad areas and lower deflector.
  - G-19, PDF page 229: rear fascia and its split lower bumper-pad / central plate arrangement. The listing distinguishes the 1985 2.8L non-WU2 fascia from other variants.
  - G-10, PDF page 309: pedestal spoiler outline and its relationship to the notchback rear deck.
  - H-7, PDF page 331: V56 carrier with longitudinal slats and an integral raised rear wing. This replaces the earlier generic tubular rack approximation.
- [Owner-sale 1985 SE V6 gallery](https://davidsclassiccars.com/pontiac/465757-1985-pontiac-fiero-se-v-6.html): supplementary front, rear and side angle comparison, cross-checked against the factory illustrations. The seller reports a repaint; this is not proof of original equipment, exact dimensions or this owner's build. These reference photographs are not included in the app or production assets.

## Geometry changes

- Replaced separate, sharply meeting end-cap panels with continuous rounded fascia surfaces whose corners turn into the side panels.
- Reshaped the nose's sloping upper surface and rolled-under lower edge; moved the paired front pads and park/turn lamps to the lower fascia and added a wrapped upper molding.
- Rolled the rear deck edge into a forward-raked lamp panel and rounded the lower rear fascia. Added paired low bumper pads surrounding the plate area, replacing the incorrect single heavy strip across the middle.
- Changed the rear lenses to divided smoked panels with finer grids, and placed the Pontiac lettering below the lamps.
- Joined the rear side molding to the rear fascia molding and seated the deck vent slats closer to the deck surface.
- Replaced the rack's transverse cargo-bar layout with longitudinal slats and a small raised integral spoiler.
- Rebuilt the optional pedestal wing as a closed curved section with a rounded leading edge, swept/lowered tips, tapered supports and mounting feet. Lowered it relative to the earlier tall approximation.

The SE identity, bumper-pad body style, axle positions and published dimension targets remain the basis of the reconstruction. The wing remains an option preview; its presence is not inferred from the VIN or the owner's phrase “all the GT options.” No swap compatibility, original mounting-hole pattern or drag/downforce claim is established by this pass.
