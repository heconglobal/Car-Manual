# Early L44 fuel supply and vapor system

53 selectable parts or grouped sets replace the original tank and schematic feed line. There are separate scopes for the tank/retainers, pump/sender, filler/vent, feed/return/filter and vapor system. Three new vehicle selections expose the pump/sender, filler and canister/relay scopes directly.

## Factory evidence inspected

- [GM 22P CD PDF 79–80](https://fieroinfo.com/manuals/84-88_Fiero_Parts_%26_Illustrations_CD.pdf#page=79): the **1985–1986 L44** fuel-supply drawing and application table. Includes early stepped tank, distinct front/rear straps, pads, floor reinforcement, locking cam, gasket, sender, filler/vent, filter, O-rings, feed/return hoses and pipe supports. Callout 6 lists separate 1985 and 1986 senders; they are not treated as an interchangeable single verified part number.
- [GM 22P CD PDF 115–117](https://fieroinfo.com/manuals/84-88_Fiero_Parts_%26_Illustrations_CD.pdf#page=115): **V6** emissions drawing/application table. Canister, support, purge/control hoses, vapor pipe, clips and pump relay are represented. The auxiliary vapor tank is explicitly a 1987–88 application and is omitted from this original 1985 model.
- [1986 Pontiac service PDF 364 / printed 6C-3](https://fieroinfo.com/manuals/1986_Fiero_Service_Manual.pdf#page=364): L44 filter bracket arrangement, pump relay and **typical** pump/sender illustration. The pump can, outlet coupler, lower isolation, inlet strainer, sender/float and splash cup use this adjacent-year shape evidence. It does not certify the precise 1985 sender internals.
- The same manual PDF 370–371 distinguishes tank, sender, pump and canister filter as service components. No pressure-test method, fuel-handling instructions or adjacent-year service specifications were imported into the app.

Other pages were inspected to distinguish model years and engines: CD 109–114 are four-cylinder emissions illustrations and were not adopted as V6 fitment; CD 118–121 are four-cylinder exhaust systems. CD 122–123 is the V6 exhaust source for subsequent work.

## Shapes and relationships

The tank has a raised rear sender area, sloping upper transition, shallow lower pan, seam flange, crown ribs and a real sender aperture. The two welded shell selections exist to expose internal components; they are explicitly labeled as an illustrative cutaway, not removable service halves. The pump/strainer sit inside the modeled tank envelope. The sender has support tubes, flange, cam ring, seal, splash cup, float arm and separate internal leads.

The filler remains at the driver quarter, including its branch vent, cap/seal, body isolator, couplings, clamps and ground strap. Feed and return follow distinct parallel routes at the rear of the tunnel; the in-line filter is on the passenger side. The vapor route is separate. The canister and fuel-pump relay are on the driver side of the engine compartment. All legacy authoring coordinates receive one final LHD conversion.

## Limits

Tank dimensions and capacity have not been measured from an original tank. Internal baffles/sump, original pump manufacturing details, sender resistance calibration, exact wire colors/keying, canister charcoal bed/valves and filter media are not reconstructed as verified internals. Pipe bends, hose clearances, fittings and mounting positions need direct confirmation. The external exhaust remains a preview pending the V6-specific exhaust reconstruction.

No photographic stand-in, paid asset or runtime reference scan is used. Factory manuals are linked as evidence; procedural meshes and materials are authored locally.
