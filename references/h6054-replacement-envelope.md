# H6054 replacement drawing

[CEC manufacturer drawing, revision 2](https://cecindustries.com/attachments/sealedBeams/H6054.pdf), issued November 10, 2016 and revised July 1, 2020, was inspected on September 22, 2026. Local research copy: `cec-h6054-replacement-drawing.pdf`.

| Drawing limit | Millimeters |
| --- | ---: |
| Overall width, maximum | 200.8 |
| Overall height, maximum | 142.8 |
| Overall depth, maximum | 135 |

The drawing shows three rear contact lugs, a sealed rectangular envelope, asymmetric optical regions and separate aiming reference features. It expressly says the drawing is not to scale. Its printed width pairing is inconsistent: 7.877 inches does not convert to 200.8 mm. Retain that discrepancy rather than silently resolving it.

This is evidence for a modern replacement envelope, not the factory-installed 1985 lamp or its optical tooling. Maximum limits must not be substituted for nominal manufactured dimensions. Likewise, this drawing does not verify the bucket, bezel, motor or Fiero linkage. No drawing image is imported into the viewer, and no runtime geometry was changed during regression.
