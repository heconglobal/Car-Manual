# Original 1985 instrument cluster

The 1985 Pontiac owner's manual, PDF32 / printed2C-1, establishes the original face: 85 mph speedometer on the driver's left, 6000 rpm tachometer on the right with oil pressure inside its lower sector, center coolant temperature above fuel, and ten warning/indicator positions. The 1985 manual-transmission SHIFT window is included. There is no later separate auxiliary rally-gauge pod.

GM22P illustration2P10-001 (parts CD PDF289–290) is specifically labeled1984–1985 and distinguishes the instrument-pod housing, top cover, front trim and column filler. Its adjacent1986–88 panel is not substituted. The 1985 owner's bulb table (PDF92 /6-4) specifies194 cluster illumination/warning bulbs. The five illumination positions are reconstructed using the separately identified1986 wiring schematic (PDF973 /8A-80-1); the exact1985 count/board layout is still unverified.

47 native selections add the pod, cover, trim, clear lens, carrier, substrate, connectors, mounting set, individual dial faces/pointers/rear units, odometer/trip/reset, illumination set and ten separate indicator-window/bulb pairs. The same geometry replaces the old generic two-dial box in the whole vehicle.

No photograph or scanned dial is used at runtime. Markings, needles, drum faces, lenses and warning windows are authored geometry/native text. Symbol shapes, dial graduation positions, casing dimensions, circuit substrates, wire-entry forms and mounting patterns remain reconstructions. No fabricated circuit traces or component calibration values are presented. The zeroed counters and resting pointers are preview values, not measurements from the owner's car.

The 1986 diagrams support construction research only. They are not published as verified1985 pinouts. Complete1985 instrument wiring, gauge movements/circuit boards, exact lens tooling, measured dimensions and validated cluster repair procedures remain open.
