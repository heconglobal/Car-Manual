# L44 valve gear reconstruction

2026-09-21. Identity references: GM Pontiac 22P [H-22 drawing / H-23 and H-24 tables](https://fieroinfo.com/manuals/84-88_Fiero_Parts_%26_Illustrations_P22.pdf#page=17), inspected locally at PDF pages 17–19.

The engine has twelve individual valve scopes, each containing nineteen selectable entries, including a nested nine-piece hydraulic lifter. Existing valve, spring, rocker/fulcrum, pushrod and lifter selections are joined by a spring retainer, split keeper pair, small stem oil seal, type-specific stem seal/shield, rocker stud and adjusting nut. Six paired pushrod-guide plates remain in their respective cylinder-head scopes. This adds 78 entries, many of which are small grouped service sets, not 78 unique designs.

The catalog distinguishes callout 44 (exhaust stem-oil shield) from 47 (retained intake stem seal). They are not duplicated indiscriminately across both valve types. Callouts 42/43/45 identify keys, cap and stem oil seal; 65–68 establish the paired guide, stud, arm/ball and nut. The geometry audit checks these variant memberships.

Rocker arms now use a thin formed base with an open stud slot, raised side flanges, curved contact pads and an annular spherical fulcrum. They are not aftermarket roller rockers. Poppet valves use lathed beveled head edges and curved tulip transitions. Keeper pairs use separate tapered half-cones around an open stem bore. Original retainer and nut meshes were removed from the formerly fused spring/rocker selections.

All local dimensions, installed heights, guide outlines, thread pitches and contact relationships are visual reconstructions. No valve adjustment, preload, spring rate, torque, tolerance or replacement compatibility is established by these meshes. Viewer positions are mapped to physical cylinder identities by the shared factory-reference mapping. Accurate combustion chambers, oil passages, hardened seat profiles and calibrated lifter internals remain unverified.

The final visual pass replaced a coarsely bent rocker base with sampled constant-thickness channel surfaces around an open slot, differentiated the pushrod socket from the valve contact pad, added curved pushrod ends and a grooved lifter exterior with a seat recess, and increased close-focus magnification for tiny keeper pairs. These refinements improve inspectability; exact manufactured contours and original lifter dimensions remain unverified. See [the implemented lifter construction view](lifter-internals-followup.md).
