# Fiero model-year reference library

The owner requested preservation of other-year and other-configuration findings for eventual expansion beyond the current 1985 SE 2M6. The working application still represents that original-year vehicle; the library does not enable other-year models or establish interchangeability.

The [library catalog](2026-10-01/catalog.json) records source URLs, local paths, model years, document types, page counts, sizes and SHA-256 hashes. It indexes 20 documents: 19 usable documents and one unusable archive response. Seven usable documents were already present and are retained at their original paths; twelve usable documents were newly acquired. Covers of all twelve new usable documents were opened and inspected. Complete internal content and page-by-page applicability have not been verified.

| Model year | Preserved material |
| --- | --- |
| 1984 | DIY, revised section 8A electrical troubleshooting, maintenance schedule, usable owner manual (109 pages; third printing, 10028873 C) |
| 1985 | DIY, intact owner manual, L44 emissions/drivability section 6E3, nominal specification sheets |
| 1986 | Service manual, DIY, owner manual, maintenance schedule, nominal specification sheets |
| 1987 | Service manual and owner manual |
| 1988 | Service manual, owner manual and maintenance schedule |
| 1984–1988 | Two GM parts/illustration catalog editions, with application rows retained |

The 1984 electrical cover explicitly identifies a revision to section 8A. Retain all acquired versions even when a factory publication says a revision supersedes an earlier section. The 1988 maintenance cover names both 2.5-liter and 2.8-liter engines; engine-specific rows must remain distinct when transcribed.

The first archive URL for the 1984 owner manual returned a 415-byte blank one-page PDF. That response remains preserved and marked unusable. A [separate Boomtastic acquisition](2026-10-02T00-09-12Z-1984-acquisition/acquisition.json) now provides a usable 109-page original 1984 owner manual: part 10028873 C, third printing. PDF pages 1 and 2 (cover and contents), 108 (printed service-station page 7-2) and 109 (back cover) were opened and inspected. Every PDF page renders nonblank image content; complete page sequence, individual specifications and later corrections have not been verified. The resolved acquisition gap remains in the catalog with its original status preserved.

The existing `references/1985-owners.pdf` also has no readable pages and remains outside the indexed usable count; the intact 1985 Boomtastic owner-manual scan is indexed separately. Neither unusable file may be counted as a complete source.

Two complete factory service-manual acquisition gaps remain open: 1984 S-8410P and 1985 S-8510P. The Boomtastic folder titled “1984 Factory Service Manual” currently lists only the electrical troubleshooting section; its title does not establish a complete manual. The catalog retains publisher/acquisition pointers. No paid documents have been purchased. A downloaded later-year service manual is not a substitute for checking a 1985 part or procedure's actual applicability.

Archive discovery: [FieroInfo original-manual collection](https://www.fieroinfo.com/manuals/) and [Boomtastic 1984 owner documents](https://www.boomtastic.com/files/?dir=Service+Manuals%2C+Guides%2C+and+Tips%2FOwners+Manuals%2F1984). Additional specific-year findings remain in the component reconstruction notes, the parts callout register and the multi-year applicability note. Preserve original scans locally; production builds publish only deliberately selected project-authored notes and source links.
